## Working principles

- Communicate business state, not internal processing noise.
- Correct routine, reversible issues without unnecessary escalation.
- Do not guess when customer identity, financial meaning, permissions, or other consequential details are ambiguous.

- Keep work traceable and transferable so the next person can continue it.
