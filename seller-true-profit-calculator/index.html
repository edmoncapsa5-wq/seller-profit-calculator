    How this works

    How is profit calculated?

    Selling price minus percentage deductions minus product cost and all other per-order costs.

    What do the percentage results mean?

    Profit margin is profit divided by selling price. Net return on product cost is actual profit divided by product cost after all entered deductions.

    Why isn't break-even simply cost + fees?

    Percentage fees grow as price grows, so break-even uses fixed costs ÷ (1 − total percentage rate).

    Calculation assumption: percentage deductions are applied to the entered selling price, while seller-funded discounts are treated as a separate per-order deduction. Your platform may use a different fee base; check your order statement.

    Estimate only. Marketplace fees, taxes, shipping, refunds and program-specific charges vary. Use your actual applicable figures.
